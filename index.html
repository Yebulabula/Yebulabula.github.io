Ye Mao

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Vision and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Krystian Mikolajczyk and supported by the Imperial President's Scholarship . I am currently a research intern at Meta, working on world foundation models for robot learning. My research develops generalizable geometry-aware models for understanding, reconstructing, and generating the 3D physical world. Before my PhD, I completed master's degrees at the University of Cambridge and Imperial, and a bachelor's degree at King's College London, where I first started programming.
